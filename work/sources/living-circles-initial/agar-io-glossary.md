# Agar.io Glossary

## Entity

### Cell
A player-controlled unit that moves, grows, and interacts with others.

---

### Food
Static or slow-moving particles that can be consumed to increase mass.

---

## Mechanics

### Movement
The ability of a cell to navigate the world, typically controlled by the player.

---

### Growth
Increase in size/mass through consumption.

---

### Consumption
The act of absorbing smaller entities.

---

### Split
Mechanic that divides a cell into multiple parts for strategic advantage.

---

### Merge
Recombination of split parts into a single entity.

---

### Mass Loss
Gradual reduction of size over time to balance gameplay.

---

## Interaction

### Collision
When two entities occupy overlapping space.

---

### Dominance
Rule where larger entities consume smaller ones.

---

### Evasion
Behavior of avoiding larger threats.

---

## System

### World
The bounded environment where entities exist.

---

### Tick
A discrete update step in the simulation loop.

---

### Latency
Delay between player input and system response.

---

### Server Authority
The server defines the true state of the game.

---

### Client Rendering
The visual representation of the game state.

---

## Multiplayer Concepts

### Player
A human participant controlling a cell.

---

### Session
A continuous gameplay instance.

---

### Leaderboard
Ranking of players based on size or score.

---

## Derived Concepts

### Risk/Reward
Trade-off between aggressive actions (split) and safety.

---

### Scaling
How entity size impacts performance (speed, control).

---

### Emergence
Complex behavior arising from simple rules.
